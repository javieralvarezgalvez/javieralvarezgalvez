# Javier Álvarez-Gálvez

👨‍🔬 **Computational Social Scientist | Sociology of Health | Network Science**

I am an Associate Professor at the University of Cádiz (Spain) and Director of the
Institute for Sustainable Social Development (INDESS). I lead the **CS2 DataLab
(Computational Social Science DataLab)**.

My research focuses on:
- Health inequalities and social determinants of health
- Multimorbidity and comorbidity networks
- Health misinformation and infodemiology
- Social network analysis and complex systems
- Agent-based and simulation models

I combine large-scale health data, network science, and computational methods to study
how social structures shape health outcomes.

## 🔬 Current Research Projects
