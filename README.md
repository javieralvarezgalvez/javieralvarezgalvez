# Javier Álvarez-Gálvez

👨‍🔬 **Computational Social Scientist | Health Data Science | Network Science**

I am an Associate Professor at the [University of Cádiz](https://www.uca.es/) (Spain) and Director of the [University Research Institute for Sustainable Social Development (INDESS)](https://indess.uca.es/),  where I teach research methods, data analysis and sociology of health. My main research interests are related with social/health data science, research methods, computational social science, health systems and health service research, social inequalities in (mental) health, health behaviours, and sociology of health and illness. I lead the [CS2 DataLab](https://www.cs2datalab.com).

My research focuses on:
- Health inequalities and social determinants of health
- Multimorbidity and comorbidity networks
- Health misinformation and infodemiology
- Computational Social Science & Quantitative Methods
- Health & Social Data Science
- Social network analysis and complex systems
- Agent-based and simulation models

I combine large-scale health data, network science, and computational methods to study
how social structures shape health outcomes.

## 🔬 Current & Recent Research Projects

### 🧠 HUMAIN (2025–2028)
**HUman–MAchine INteractions: Unravelling AI-driven digital inequalities and their impact on misinformation and health behaviours**  
*Principal Investigator*  
Spanish National Research Programme (€261,312)

This project examines how AI-driven digital environments contribute to social and health inequalities, with a specific focus on health misinformation, algorithmic exposure, and behavioural outcomes. The project combines computational social science, network analysis, and digital trace data to study how human–machine interactions shape health-related beliefs and behaviours.

### 🩺 DEMAND (2025–2027)
**Social Inequalities in Multimorbidity in Andalusia: Impact on Mortality, Health Services Use and Quality of Life**  
*Principal Investigator*  
Regional Health Research Programme (Junta de Andalucía) (€95,000)

This project investigates socioeconomic inequalities in multimorbidity patterns using large-scale health data. It focuses on mortality, healthcare utilization, and quality of life outcomes, applying network-based and longitudinal methods to inform health policy and planning.

### 🚭 RELIEF (2025–2027)
**InteRvening EarLier In life for hEalthier liFestyles**  
*Research Collaborator*  
EU4Health Action Grant (€784,100)

A multinational project aimed at reducing exposure to and use of emerging tobacco products among school children. The project integrates behavioural science, public health interventions, and cross-country comparative analyses.

### 🌐 NETDYNAMIC (2023–2025)
**Misinformation ecosystems during public health crises**  
*Principal Investigator*  
Spanish National Research Programme (€199,793)

NETDYNAMIC analyses how health misinformation spreads across digital platforms during public health crises, combining social network analysis, text mining, and computational modelling to study infodemic dynamics and their societal consequences.

### 🦠 DCODES (2021–2024)
**Collective dynamics of health opinion contagion: the COVID-19 infodemic and decision-making processes**  
*Principal Investigator*  
Spanish National Research Programme (€163,246)

This project explored opinion dynamics, social contagion, and behavioural responses during the COVID-19 pandemic using agent-based models, network science, and survey data.

### 🧬 DEMMOCAD (2020–2023)
**Determinants of physical and mental multimorbidity patterns by neighbourhood**  
*Principal Investigator*  
ITI Programme (€299,908)

A large-scale project focused on identifying and characterising complex multimorbidity patterns and their social determinants using latent class analysis, self-organising maps, and network models.

### 🧩 SDoH (2017–2022)
**Social Determinants of Health**  
*Principal Investigator (Ramon & Cajal Programme)*  
Ministry of Economy and European Social Fund (€308,000)

A research line dedicated to understanding how structural and social determinants shape health inequalities, health behaviours, and access to healthcare across populations.

Together, these projects form a coherent research agenda on health inequalities, multimorbidity, and digital health in Europe.

## 🧠 Methods & Tools

### Computational & Statistical Methods
- Multivariate and longitudinal analysis
- Mixed Graphical Models (MGM) and regularized regression (LASSO)
- Network analysis and complex systems approaches
- Meta-analysis and stratified methods (e.g. Cochran–Mantel–Haenszel)
- Text mining, NLP, and digital trace data analysis
- Agent-based modelling and simulation of social dynamics

### Programming & Software
- **R**: tidyverse, igraph, netdiffuseR, mgm, qgraph, lme4, survival, ggplot2  
- **Python**: pandas, numpy, networkx, scikit-learn, Mesa  
- **Simulation**: NetLogo, Mesa (Python)  
- **Statistical software**: Stata, R, Python

### Network Science & Modelling
- Construction and analysis of comorbidity and social networks
- Temporal and multilayer networks
- Centrality, community detection, and diffusion processes
- Opinion dynamics and behavioural contagion models

### Reproducible & Open Science
- Reproducible workflows using **RMarkdown** and **Jupyter Notebooks**
- Version control and collaborative development with **GitHub**
- Modular analysis pipelines for multi-country and multi-database studies
- Aggregated-data sharing protocols for privacy-preserving research
